## Caveats

Annual labour-force data in the registered source end in 2023. No 2024 estimate is substituted. GDP per capita is nominal and the registered workbook supplies 2023–2025. Productivity is the published DOSM series, not a ratio constructed using labour-force survey employment. Kuala Lumpur productivity includes Putrajaya. GDP at constant prices and nominal household income cannot be treated as the same concept. The 2020 modelled income estimates are excluded from actual survey trends. No causal attribution is made.
